🤔 Troubleshooting & FAQ

Why can't my AI agent see my browser?

Make sure both BrowserSkill and your AI agent are running on the same computer. Also check that the browser extension is enabled (look for the BrowserSkill icon in your browser's toolbar).

Is my data safe?

Yes. BrowserSkill only runs on your local machine. Your login credentials and personal information never leave your computer unless you explicitly ask the AI to share them.

Does BrowserSkill work with any website?

Yes, it can interact with any website that works in a standard browser. Some complex sites (e.g., those requiring 2FA) might need additional setup, but most sites work out of the box.

Can I use BrowserSkill with multiple AI agents?

Absolutely! You can connect different AI agents to BrowserSkill one at a time, or even run multiple sessions simultaneously. Each gets its own isolated workspace.

Does it slow down my computer?

Not noticeably. BrowserSkill uses minimal CPU when idle, and even during active automation, it's lighter than a typical video call.
